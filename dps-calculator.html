DPS Calculator

iAbout this beta

A turn-by-turn simulation of your battle party in one raid, using simple rules. Use it to compare setups - it is a guide, not a prediction of a real run.

Your party

  • A main DPSer, up to five support DPSers, and up to three kinds of catch tank - or no catch tanks at all (Remove on Catch Tank 1), for hot swapping only.
  • A catch tank swaps in as the boss's charge move lands, uses one fast move, fires the charge moves set on its card, and swaps back. With Double catch, it catches twice: after the first it uses the set fast moves and swaps out holding its energy, then comes back for the boss's next charge move and fires on both catches' energy (only when it has the HP for the second catch).
  • Swaps take 0.5s. Each swap to a catch tank is timed for the highest DPS: the fight is run for every swap time, 0.5s apart, and the best is kept.

The boss

  • It starts with 0 energy and gains it from its fast moves and from half the damage it takes, up to 100. On each move it has the aggression slider's chance of using its charge move once it has that move's energy (33 for a 3-bar move, 50 for a 2-bar, 100 for a 1-bar), and it never casts two in a row. Each Calculate is a new random fight.

Who takes each charge move

  • Priority & hot swap order lists the attackers in the order they come in, and under each one, in order, who takes the boss's charge moves while it is in: its own catch tanks and its own sponge. Soft lets an attacker borrow an unused catch tank from another before it hot swaps; Strict keeps each to its own.
  • Sponging follows the situation. While the attacker has catch tanks, it only sponges when it absorbs at least 90% of the hit's energy (no more than 10% overflow), survives, and can fire before the boss's next charge move - otherwise a catch tank takes it. With none left, it sponges whenever its HP covers another full rotation: the hit, then the boss's fast moves while it builds and casts its next charge move.
  • Can dodge (on each attacker): it dodges instead, up to Dodges before bailing, when it keeps at least 85% of the dodged hit's energy, survives it, and can fire before the boss's next charge move. A dodge takes 25% of the damage and 1.0s.

Hot swaps

  • An attacker hot swaps to the next one when it can't safely take the boss's next charge move (no catch tank left, and a sponge or dodge isn't safe), or when it can't live to land its next charge move. It fires the charge move it is building first if that lands in time - or stays in, takes the hit, and lands it, when it can survive to. The next attacker comes in with full HP and no energy; attackers never come back.
  • Doubling back: when an incoming charge move would make the attacker in overflow and a support DPSer could absorb it fully, the simulator plays the fight forward both ways and swaps to the support if that deals more damage. The support is used until it is done, then the attacker comes back - with the HP and energy it left with - ahead of any new one.
  • Juice main DPS: before hot swapping, the attacker goes in for one more charge move whenever the boss's moves let it live to land it - the gamble a run needing every last charge move takes. When they wouldn't, it hot swaps as if it never tried.

The end of the run

  • The run ends when the last attacker can't continue. With All in final charge move it stays in on the energy it holds - dodging or taking the hit - and the run ends when that charge move lands (or the last one before it, if it faints first). Without it, it bails, and results stop at its last charge move.

Rejoins and results

  • Rejoin time (7.0s by default, down to 5.5s) is how long each rejoin takes. Expected rejoins is how many the party needs before the 300s timer runs out, counting each rejoin's own time: 98.5s cycles with 7.0s rejoins need 2, as the third cycle runs past 300s. Minimum DPS and Cumulative DPS allow for those rejoins.
  • The result shows each attacker's and catch tank's DPS and time in, a timeline of every move, and a tick-by-tick CSV.

Not modelled yet

  • Multi-player Buffs, including the Primal / Mega boost (switched off in this mode, and hidden).
  • Resolve Nerf; dodging fast moves; dodging by catch tanks; trailing catch tanks.
