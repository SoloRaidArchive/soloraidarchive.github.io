Section 1.0: Boss Stats

Bosses are assigned CPMs, flat health and gain 100% IV's. External modifiers, such as weather boost and shadow boost, are applied outside of their effective stats.

1.0.0: Tier 4-5:

  1. Tier 4: 9000 HP, 0.79 CPM, 300s
  2. Tier 5: 15000 HP, 0.79 CPM, 300s

1.0.1: Tiers 6+

  1. Tier 6: 22500 HP, 0.79 CPM, 300s
  2. Elite: 25000 HP, 1.00 CPM, 300s

1.0.2: Shadow Tier 5

  1. Shadow T5: 15000 HP, 0.82 CPM, Shadow Debuff, 300s
  2. eff_def = (base_def+15)*0.82
  3. eff_atk = (base_atk+15)*0.82
  4. Shadow T5 Enraged: Adds 2.2x effective defense & 0.8x effective attack.
  5. enr_def = floor(eff_def + 2.2*eff_def) ≈ 3.2x Defense
  6. enr_atk = floor(eff_atk + 0.8*eff_atk) ≈ 1.8x Attack
  7. Shadow Buff additionally applies a 20% increase in attack and 20% decrease in defense. This applies outside the internal stats and on the same layer as other modifiers such as Weather Boost, Blade Boost etc.
  8. shadow_enr_def = enr_def * 0.8333
  9. shadow_enr_atk = enr_atk * 1.2
  10. shadow_eff_def = eff_def * 0.8333
  11. shadow_eff_atk = eff_atk * 1.2

1.0.3: Super Mega Raids (T7)

  1. Super Mega T7: 25000 HP, 0.79 CPM, 300s
  2. Super Mega T7: (Enraged): Gains 3x base defense and 0.5x attack
  3. enr_def = floor(eff_def + 3.0*eff_def) ≈ 4x Defense
  4. enr_atk = floor(eff_atk + 0.5*eff_atk) ≈ 1.5x Attack

1.0.4: Enrage Stat Activation & Subdues

  1. Shadow Enrage activates once a boss reaches ≤ 60% health and subdues when the boss reaches ≤ 15% health.
  2. Super Mega Enrage activates once a boss reaches ≤ 80% health and does not subdue until enough mega charge moves are used.
  3. Shadow Tier 5 bosses when subdued gain a defense and attack drop. This is currently unknown.
  4. Passover damage through the threshold is calculated with the boss' stats at cast time, not during landing time. This allows carefully planned hit timings prior to enrage.

Section 1.1: Raid Boss Attack Patterns

1.1.0: Raid Boss base attack pattern

Raid bosses randomly wait 1.5 or 2.0s until casting the next move. The probability of the picking either delay appears to be 50%.

  1. FM Cadence = Rand(1.5s ,2.0s) + FM_AD
  2. CM Cadence = Rand(1.5s ,2.0s) + CM_AD
  3. CM only occurs when boss has enough energy.

1.1.1: Post CM Chained attack

Bosses always chain an attack after casting their charge move. A chained attack is casted precisely 0.5s, 1 turn, after the previous charge move attack lands.

  1. Chained_FM = 0.5s + FM_AD
  2. Chained_CM = 0.5s + CM_AD
  3. Chained_CM only occurs when boss has enough energy.

1.1.2: Raid Boss beginning attack pattern:

At the start of the fight the first two fast moves come out quicker than normal. This phenomena does not reoccur during the rest of the raid.

Section 1.2: Energy Absorption, Rounding, Dodging

1.2.0: Boss and player energy absorption

  1. 50% of damage taken is gained as energy, rounded up. E.g: 45 damage → 23 energy
  2. 50% of damage taken by the boss is also given as energy.

1.2.1: Energy absorption timings

  1. Energy is absorbed by the boss upon player move landing. Meaning the last turn of a move.
  2. Energy is absorbed by the player upon boss move landing. Meaning the last turn of a move.
  3. Energy gained from casting fast moves by the player or boss resolves after the first turn. E.g: a fast move psycho cut gains energy on 0.5s when casted at 0.0s.

1.2.2: Dodging

  1. Dodging is a considered as a player action with animation duration 1.0s (2t).
  2. If dodge is applied within the animation duration but not the landing tick of an incoming charge move, it will reduce damage and energy received by 75%.
  3. Dodging within boss move cooldown...
  4. will apply on the next fast move.
  5. It will not apply if that next move is a charge move; dodge must be performed within the charge move animation duration (after or when text appears)
  6. Dodging prevents the active pokemon from taking any action until the end of its animation duration.
  7. Possible Bug: Dodging appears to delay the energy resolve of the pokemon's next fast move. This makes it seemingly impossible to dodge & then immediately TPC.

Section 1.3: Chat & Overhead Text

1.3.0: Charge Move Text

  1. Charge move attack text seems to appear at the end of the first turn of its animation duration.
  2. Party power text seems to appear at the end of the first turn of a charge move animation duration.

1.3.1: Environmental Boosts

  1. Adventure effect, Mega text and weather text are displayed upon entering the arena. Both are redisplayed when re-joining the lobby from a heal.
  2. Weather text is additionally re-displayed when a swap occurs. But adventure effect text is not.
  3. Mega text is additionally re-displayed when a swap occurs but only if the lobby contains more than 1 active player.

1.3.2: Overhead Texts

  1. Effectiveness texts for both the player and boss are displayed upon hit landing. This seems to occur on the beginning of the final turn of the casted move.
