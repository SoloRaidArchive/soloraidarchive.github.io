What the numbers mean

Catch Tank DPS

Standard Catch Tank rotation — It calculates fast move(s) and charge move damage used over total time; including forward and backward swap time

Trailing DPS

Unique Exit Rotation. It calculates fast move(s) and charge move damage used over total time; only including forward swap time

Entries

The same Pokémon can appear several times against the same boss at different levels, IVs, weather, or adventure effects — each counts separately.
